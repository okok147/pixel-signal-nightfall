# Nightfall Meadow — Art Direction

## Intent

Create an original cozy Celtic-fantasy survivor game: approachable, handmade, warm and readable even when the screen is crowded. The emotional target is **a gentle fantasy-life world suddenly becoming busy at night**.

This direction may borrow broad genre qualities associated with classic fantasy-life MMORPGs—soft cel-shaded forms, pastoral landscapes, warm wood and parchment UI, rounded characters and Celtic motifs—but must not copy Mabinogi characters, costumes, logos, interface layouts, icons, textures, maps or named assets.

## Visual pillars

1. **Soft fantasy life** — grass, flowers, stones, sheep-like creatures, lanterns, moon dew, tiny domestic objects.
2. **Cute readable combat** — large face shapes, distinct silhouettes, thick dark outlines, one bright gameplay color per unit.
3. **Warm handmade UI** — parchment cards, timber frames, stitched tabs, hand-lettered-feeling typography, very little chrome.
4. **Quiet field, loud actions** — scenery stays muted; attacks, XP and danger use saturated mint, coral, gold and lavender.
5. **Clarity under pressure** — no decorative detail may hide enemies, projectiles, pickups or the player.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Night ink | `#21192B` | outlines, deepest UI edges |
| Night violet | `#17152B` | overlays and pause veil |
| Meadow | `#273B36` | main field |
| Parchment | `#F6E8BD` | cards and dialogue panels |
| Cream | `#FFF2C9` | headings and player clothing |
| Mint | `#8EE3C2` | XP, friendly magic, pickups |
| Lavender | `#BFA7FF` | player scarf, rare magic |
| Coral | `#FF758F` | health, danger, damage |
| Peach | `#FFB98B` | flowers, warm highlights |
| Gold | `#FFD56A` | treasure, evolution, fireflies |
| Timber | `#6F4938` | frames and equipment slots |

Keep backgrounds below roughly 55% perceived brightness. The player should always contain cream plus one saturated accent. Standard enemies should use one body hue; elite enemies may use two.

## Shape language

- Player: 14–18 pixel-wide core silhouette, head approximately 40% of total height, visible scarf or cape, two bright eye pixels.
- Small enemy: low rounded body, readable face, one asymmetrical feature.
- Elite enemy: wider than tall, horns/ears/pack shape, stronger ground shadow.
- Pickups: five-pixel star/diamond family with a white center.
- Projectiles: never circular blobs only; use stars, leaves, notes, needles or runes.
- UI corners: clipped pixel corners rather than perfectly rounded vector rectangles.

## HUD contract

- Gameplay occupies almost the whole screen.
- Top-left: portrait + health only.
- Top-center: survival timer only.
- Top-right: level, kills, chests.
- Bottom-center: five compact loadout slots.
- Bottom edge: one thin full-width XP bar.
- No permanent control legend during play.
- No giant top dashboard, debug language or sci-fi telemetry.

## Upgrade cards

Each card contains, in order:

1. small category label;
2. 48–72 px icon;
3. short friendly name;
4. one-sentence mechanical effect;
5. keyboard number.

Use parchment background and dark berry text. Evolution cards add gold trim but keep the same structure.

## Animation rules

- Idle: 1–2 px breathing/bobbing, 1.2–1.8 second cycle.
- Walk: clear two-step rhythm; avoid smooth skating.
- Hit: 70–100 ms white flash plus 1–2 px recoil.
- Pickup: quick curved travel to player, then one small star burst.
- Level up: freeze gameplay, soften background, cards rise 8–12 px.
- Chest: anticipation pause, lid pop, radial gold motes, reward text.

## Asset checklist

### Characters
- Meadow Courier: idle, walk 4-direction, hurt, defeat.
- Dusk Slime: idle, hop, hurt, defeat.
- Moonhorn: idle, charge, hurt, defeat.
- Later: wool sprite, mushroom thief, lantern moth, hedge witch.

### Combat
- Star Spark projectile and hit.
- Fairy Flame orbit and hit.
- Moon Dew pickup.
- Protective Pulse ring.
- Story Chest closed/open.

### UI
- portrait frame;
- health/XP/pulse bars;
- five equipment slots;
- parchment card normal/hover/rare;
- 12 weapon/passive icons;
- pause, result and toast panels.

## Do not do

- Do not use copied Mabinogi screenshots or extracted files.
- Do not reproduce its logo, exact UI proportions, named races, costumes or iconography.
- Do not return to neon cyan sci-fi grids.
- Do not use tiny thin fonts over textured backgrounds.
- Do not make the player and enemies share the same silhouette.
- Do not add detail simply to make the screen look expensive.
